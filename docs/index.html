speakEZ

A case study · 2021–2023

Shipped · funded · finished

A social app for 150 friends. Not a following.

Group chat and a social feed in one room, with a door that opened into real life. It would not make you famous. It did not care if you were verified.

The loop

  1. 01

    You make a Moment — text, photo, video, or audio.

  2. 02

    A Lock picks who sees it. Saved lists, reused next time.

  3. 03

    It lands on their Timeline.

  4. 04

    A comment hoists it back to the top. Hot ones rise.

The product

Try 151 and you run into trouble.

Moments
The post. As many as you want. Text, photo, video, or audio.
Locks
Saved privacy lists. Close Friends as a first-class object, not a toggle.
Timeline
Commenting hoists a moment. Conversation keeps it alive, not a clock.
Messages
One-to-one and group chat. Offline send and retry.
Events
Plan something in the real world, share a link, request to join.
The rest
Push, read receipts, view-once, silence, phone auth, themes.

The story

I built speakEZ because I was watching real friendships fade. High school and university people turning into memories. Instagram and Snapchat were full of the world and empty of the people I actually knew.

I was the lead developer, the designer, and the company. I wrote the first version alone and shipped 1.03 to the App Store on March 20, 2021 — about eight weeks after I started. I was still learning to program.

speakEZ Inc. was incorporated that July. A Kickstarter in February 2022 asked for CA$10,000 to keep the lights on. One hundred and eight people pledged CA$11,855. At the time I said we had about 400 users.

Version 2.12 shipped a fake first friend so new accounts were not empty.

Ahmad built the engine that made it feel fast. Sultan did push notifications. Jack stood up the first cloud functions as a favour, three days, before I had even started the iPhone app. After Ahmad left in July 2022 I finished it alone: events, a theme system, a new login, version 5.11 on January 13, 2023.

I had already started the next one. It never went out. The company was later dissolved. The listing is still up.
